How the numbers work

  • You pay 12% of basic + DA every month.
  • Your employer also pays 12%, but 8.33% (of wages up to ₹15,000) goes to the pension scheme (EPS). The rest goes into your EPF.
  • Interest is worked out every month on the running balance and added at year end.
  • The salary increase applies from the start of each new year.
  • Rates and rules can change, so check with EPFO.
